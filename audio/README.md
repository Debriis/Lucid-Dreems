# Audio

Original supplied OGGs, original lullaby.wav, ambience beds and supplied sound
effects are preserved. The canonical story winds each box backward: pitch
slows, a reversed phrase plays once, then stops. unwind_reverse.wav is derived
from the first three seconds of the supplied spooky waltz using Godot audio
decoding in tools/render_reverse.gd. The fourth box calls Game.silence().

Existing winding, rattle and dream cues remain connected. The appointment
clock uses occasional paired UI-click cues. The ending stops its flatline cue,
cuts to black and plays exactly one click without restarting the music.
Credits and source licenses remain in assets/fever_dreams/.
