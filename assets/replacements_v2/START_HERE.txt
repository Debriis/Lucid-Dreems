Natural-color head bases are included as optional alternatives.
All character and prop PNGs have transparency. These are flat sprites with a low-poly rendered look, not 3D models.
Sprites are individual illustrations, not a registered animation sheet. Align/scale the open and closed states in your scene before swapping them.
Keep PNG alpha on import. Use nearest-neighbor filtering for the intended rough pixels.
UI is designed around a 640 x 480 canvas. Blank panels do not contain text. See ui/TYPE_SETTINGS.txt for font weight and layout. Editable SVG sources are included.
The original asset pack remains available for backgrounds, textures, music and sound effects. This folder contains the requested replacement files only.

PROVENANCE
Character and music-box PNGs created with the built-in image-generation tool. Identity references were supplied by the user. Prompts are recorded in notes/.
UI components are original editable vector artwork. The example incorporates the earlier cabin composition as a preview backdrop.
