# Fever Dreams asset sources

Collected for this jam on 30 October 2026. This handoff contains assets, not a playable game.

## Textures and room construction

- **Seamless Abstract Pack — Screaming Brain Studios.** https://screamingbrainstudios.itch.io/seamless-abstract-pack . CC0. All 50 patterns at 128, 256 and 512 pixels, in `textures/abstract_128`, `abstract_256`, and `abstract_512`. Each edition's original license is retained. Credit optional.
- **Old School Dungeon Crawler Pack — Screaming Brain Studios.** https://screamingbrainstudios.itch.io/dungeon-crawler-pack . CC0. Base brick perspective pieces in `textures/dungeon_perspective`; cave add-on in `textures/dungeon_caves`. Original license files included. Credit optional.
- **Retro Textures Fantasy — Kenney.** https://kenney.nl/assets/retro-textures-fantasy . CC0. Original PNG files in `textures/kenney_fantasy`, with the original license. Credit optional.
- The assembled room backgrounds are new compositions made with these texture sources and original geometry.

## Music box

- **Music Box, Swiss, circa 1820 — The Metropolitan Museum of Art.** https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/505671 . Object 1976.285.30a, b; Gift of Murtogh D. Guinness, 1976. The museum marks its image Public Domain. The unmodified photo is included in `props/music_box_original.jpg`.
- `props/music_box_cutout.png` is an AI-assisted transparent-background edit using the built-in image-generation tool. Its prompt and provenance are in `props/MUSIC_BOX_EDIT_PROMPT.txt`.

## Audio

- **4 Music Box Tracks — Aureolus_Omicron.** https://opengameart.org/content/4-music-box-tracks . CC0; author explicitly says no credit required. All four OGG tracks and MIDI source files are included.
- `audio/sfx/` contains original synthesized nonverbal cues created for this project: UI click, winding key, spring release, mechanical rattle, chase pulse, dream transition, and session termination. These cues may be used and modified for the game.

## Font

- **DotGothic16 — The DotGothic16 Project Authors / Fontworks.** https://github.com/fontworks-fonts/DotGothic16 . SIL Open Font License 1.1. Keep `fonts/OFL.txt` with any distribution containing the font.

## Characters and interface

- Purple animatronic: custom low-poly construction based on the user's supplied visual reference. Ayush witch head and patient: custom low-poly constructions using the respective photos supplied by the user. Those photo likenesses are project-specific material, separate from the CC0 stock packs above.
- Dialogue panels and cursors are original vector geometry made for the project, provided as editable SVG and transparent PNG.
- Optional `dialogue.json` is original fictional story material. The patient role is fictional and is not a claim about the photographed person.

Suggested compact credits: Textures by Screaming Brain Studios and Kenney; music by Aureolus_Omicron; music-box photograph courtesy of The Metropolitan Museum of Art; DotGothic16 by The DotGothic16 Project Authors. Character likenesses supplied by the team.
