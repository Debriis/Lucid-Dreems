# Four music boxes. One very bad dream. Meet The Last Lala-Bye

Published: https://sammy-ryed.itch.io/the-last-lala-bye/devlog/1740140/four-music-boxes-one-very-bad-dream-meet-the-last-lala-bye

**Your patient keeps saying he's fine. The house disagrees.**

The Last Lala-Bye started with three game-jam constraints: a music box, an animatronic, and a witch's cabin. Somehow, that became a sleep doctor walking into someone else's nightmare while a giant floating head tries to kill them.

You enter a patient's recurring dream to find out what he can't say while he's awake. Four music boxes are still playing. Unwind them, read what's been left behind, and keep moving. Every room has a clock, and the house gets impatient.

## Meet the things waiting for you

**RUE / THE PERFORMER** is the patient's childhood teddy, rebuilt by the dream as a purple animatronic bunny. You'll meet him as soon as you arrive. Stay close when things get bad: the creepy rabbit is on your side.

**AYUSH / THE KEEPER** is the witch who thinks keeping someone safe means never letting them leave. His cabin is the heart of the dream. He has a room of his own, a face that opens much further than it should, and very strong opinions about visitors.

## Something familiar underneath the noise

Under the wrong colours, grainy textures and broken lullabies, this is a story about unanswered messages, drifting away from people, and saying “I'm fine” because explaining feels harder. We wanted the dream to feel personal, even when it looks completely ridiculous.

The look takes inspiration from LSD: Dream Emulator and old PS1 games: low-poly characters, photographic faces, ugly wallpaper, and rooms that feel slightly impossible. Yes, we put our friends' faces in the nightmare.

The current browser build includes four dream rooms, readable notes, dialogue choices with different replies, room timers, health, and Rue's protection. Reading pauses the room clock, so take your time with the notes. Surviving the walk between them is another matter.
