# The Last Lullaby

## Short description

Your patient can't wake up. His childhood toy says you shouldn't.

## Page description

**He keeps saying he's fine. The house knows he isn't.**

You are a sleep doctor entering a patient's recurring nightmare. Inside a witch's cabin, familiar rooms have twisted into places he cannot leave. Four music boxes hold the dream together. Turn their keys backward, hear what they remember, and open the next door.

**Rue**, his childhood teddy bunny rebuilt as a purple animatronic, can protect you. **Ayush, the Keeper**, believes the only way to keep him safe is to keep him here forever.

Stay near Rue when the witch approaches. Each room gives you two minutes; after that, the house starts taking your health. Find three optional scraps of an unfinished message to learn what the patient has been trying to say.

- Four dream rooms, including a witch's cabin and Ayush's chamber.
- Music-box unwinding, pursuit, health, room timers and checkpoint retries.
- Rue's protective shield and an optional distraction ability.
- Different replies to dialogue choices; optional notes affect the final conversation.
- Grainy low-resolution scenery, photographic character textures and torn-paper menus.
- Spoken patient dialogue and distinct invented voices for the other characters.

A short, single-session psychological-horror jam game made in Godot.

### Controls

WASD to move. Mouse to look. Q/C or left/right arrows to turn. E, Enter or Space to interact. W/S or arrows to select dialogue. Esc to pause.

### Play in your browser

Click **Run Game**, then click inside the game to enable sound and mouse controls. Play on a desktop or laptop with a keyboard and mouse; fullscreen is recommended. No installation needed. Reloading resets story progress.

### Content and settings

Contains pursuit, sudden loud sounds, distorted imagery, and a fictional story about depression, isolation and emotional pressure. Music, voice and effects have separate volume controls. Reduced Effects reduces distortion and removes random glimpse scares; catch scares remain.
